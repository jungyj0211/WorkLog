    - 영문: On-Device SMPS Remaining Useful Life Estimation Framework with a Damage-Accumulation Model
  - 저자 : 정용진(1저자), 오승준(공동), 김성율(공동), 전민호(공동), 오창헌(교신)
  - **Abstract :**
  This study proposes an On-Device Integrated Framework designed to overcome several key challenges in industrial SMPS prognostics and health management, including constrained computing resources on MCUs, the lack of labeled failure data, and limitations in accessing internal sensors. The framework strategically integrates two lightweight models. First, an Unsupervised Anomaly Detection Model, based on a Lightweight Autoencoder, processes external sensor signals (FFT, voltage, current) to quantify deviation as an Anomaly Score. Second, to bypass the complexity of high-cost recurrent models like LSTM, a Cumulative Damage RUL Estimation Model is used. This model combines the anomaly score with the case temperature, utilizing the Arrhenius Model and Miner's Rule for reliable RUL prediction. The proposed solution aims to achieve deployment without equipment modification, supporting efficient maintenance decision-making through integrated online update and automated threshold tuning strategies.
